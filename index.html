The Art of Levantine Confectionery

Discover the passion, pure ingredients, and timeless traditions behind Nafeeseh.

A Legacy of Levantine Taste. Reimagined in Luxury.

Long before it became a tradition, confectionery in the Levant was an act of pure devotion—a way to welcome guests, celebrate milestones, and honor generations of family gathering. Founded on the bustling streets of Amman, Nafeeseh began with a simple yet uncompromising vision: to master the timeless art of oriental sweets while elevating every detail for the modern connoisseur. Today, that foundational spirit lives on in every layer of flaky pastry, every drop of aromatic orange-blossom syrup, and every hand-selected green pistachio.

Pure Ingredients

We source 100% pure golden ghee, hand-harvested Syrian pistachios, premium cashews, and fresh local dairy daily.

Masterful Craft

Generations of master pastry chefs passing down secret techniques in dough layering and syrup balance.

Timeless Generosity

Every box is packed with warmth and hospitality, designed to make family gatherings unforgettable.
